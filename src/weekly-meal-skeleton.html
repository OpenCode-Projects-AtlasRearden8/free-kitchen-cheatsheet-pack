Weekly Meal Skeleton Planner

Flexible themes, not rigid recipes — plan in five gentle minutes

How the Skeleton Works

  1. Pick a theme per night, not a recipe. "Pasta night" can be forty different meals.
  2. Anchor recipes you already make well to their themes. Two favorites per theme is plenty.
  3. Budget one cooking night and one leftovers night — the skeleton absorbs the chaos.
  4. Leave one empty night for pizza-or-pantry night. Zero guilt is part of the plan.
  5. Shop from the themes, not the dinners: cheese + pasta + jar sauce + protein covers a pasta night.
