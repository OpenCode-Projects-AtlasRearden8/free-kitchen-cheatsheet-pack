Ingredient Substitutions

You're out of something — here's what to reach for instead

Dairy & Fats

You need…Use instead (1 : 1 unless noted)
Butter, bakingEqual coconut oil, or ¾ cup oil per 1 cup butter
Buttermilk1 cup milk + 1 Tbsp lemon juice or vinegar; rest 5 min
Sour cream / yogurtSwap for each other, or pureed cottage cheese
Heavy cream, for sauce¾ cup milk + ¼ cup melted butter
Milk, in bakingEqual water + 1 tsp butter per cup; or watered yogurt

Pantry Baking

You need…Use instead
1 tsp baking powder¼ tsp baking soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar (or ⅓ tsp soda + acidic liquid in recipe)
1 tsp baking soda2–3 tsp baking powder (omit recipe salt)
1 cup cake flour1 cup AP flour − 2 Tbsp, + 2 Tbsp cornstarch
1 cup self-rising flour1 cup AP flour + 1½ tsp baking powder + ¼ tsp salt
Honey / sugar swapHoney is sweeter: use ¾ cup honey for 1 cup sugar, cut liquid 2 Tbsp, lower oven 25 °F
Cornstarch2 Tbsp flour per 1 Tbsp cornstarch (cook longer)

Herbs, Spices & Flavor

You need…Use instead
Fresh herb (1 Tbsp)1 tsp dried, added earlier in cooking
Dried herb (1 tsp)1 Tbsp fresh, added at the end
Lemon juiceVinegar or lime juice, equal amount
Garlic clove½ tsp jarred minced or ⅛ tsp powder
Fresh ginger¼ tsp ground per 1 Tbsp fresh
WorcestershireSoy sauce + splash of vinegar + pinch sugar
Dijon mustard1 tsp yellow mustard + pinch cayenne

Grains, Pasta & Meat

You need…Use instead
QuinoaAny grain cooked 1 : 2 with water (couscous, rice, millet)
Panko or breadcrumbs (1 cup)Crushed crackers, or ¾ cup oats + ¼ cup flour
Ground beefEqual ground turkey (add 1 tsp oil), or lentils up to half
Chicken stockBouillon: 1 cube or 1 tsp granules per 1 cup hot water
Golden rule: match the function — moisture, fat, lift, acid, or binding — not the exact name. A sour cream swap that tastes different but bakes the same is a win.
