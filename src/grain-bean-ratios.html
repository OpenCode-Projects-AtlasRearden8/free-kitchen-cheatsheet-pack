Safety must-knows: Baking soda can speed softening, but it destroys thiamine and turns mushy — skip it. Salt late in cooking; salt from the start can keep skins firm (some cooks like it). Kidney beans in particular must reach a full rolling boil for at least 10 minutes to break down their natural toxin (raw kidney beans contain more of it; slow-cookers at low temps can leave it intact — boil first, then simmer or pressure-cook). Fava beans can cause serious illness in anyone with G6PD deficiency (favism). Bay leaves, onion, and a strip of kombu are kind companions.
