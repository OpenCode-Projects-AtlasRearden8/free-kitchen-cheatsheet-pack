Kitchen Conversions

Measure with confidence — volume, weight, and temperature, all on one page

Spoons & Cups → Tablespoons

  • 3 tsp = 1 Tbsp
  • 2 Tbsp = 1 fl oz
  • 1 fl oz = 29.6 mL (call it 30 mL)
  • 4 Tbsp = ¼ cup
  • 5⅓ Tbsp = ⅓ cup
  • 8 Tbsp = ½ cup
  • 10⅔ Tbsp = ⅔ cup
  • 12 Tbsp = ¾ cup
  • 16 Tbsp = 1 cup
