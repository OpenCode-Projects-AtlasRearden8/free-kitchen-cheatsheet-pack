Welcome to Your Kitchen Cheatsheets

Seven friendly one-page helpers for confident home cooking

Hi there, cook. These pages were made for real kitchens — the kind with flour on the counter and a timer you can't find. Print them, keep them on the fridge or inside a cabinet door, and cook with a little more calm.

What's in the pack

  • Kitchen Conversions — cups, spoons, grams, and oven temps.
  • Ingredient Substitutions — what to reach for when you're out of something.
  • Grain & Bean Ratios — how much water, how long, what it should look like.
  • Safe Thawing & Storage — the food-safety rules that matter, in plain English.
  • Freezer Label Log — date it, name it, find it later.
  • Weekly Meal Skeleton — flexible dinner planning, not rigid scheduling.
  • Knife Basics — grips, cuts, and care for safer prep.

Printing tips

  • Print on US Letter, portrait, at 100% scale ("Actual size" — not "fit to page").
  • Cardstock or 32 lb paper holds up better on the fridge; sheet protectors work too.
  • The label log and meal planner are meant to be written on — print several copies.
These sheets are for general home cooking education. They are not medical or dietary advice — when in doubt about food safety or health questions, ask your doctor or your local extension office.
